How it works

Use it alongside any game, real or online.

Pick your side

Choose White or Black when the app opens.

Enter their move

Click the board or type it: e4, Nf3, O-O.

Get your move

A blue arrow shows the best reply, with the evaluation and depth.

Repeat

Play it, enter their answer, and keep going. Undo any mistake.

Install in a minute

Needs Python 3.8+. For full strength, add Stockfish to the folder.

git clone https://github.com/skez01/chess-advisor.git cd chess-advisor pip install -r requirements.txt python chess_advisor.py

Power users can tune the engine:

python chess_advisor.py --threads 8 --hash 2048 --tablebases ~/syzygy --book master.bin

FAQ

Will it never lose?

No engine can promise that. Chess isn't solved, and top engines still draw each other often. Stockfish at full strength is far stronger than any human, but results also depend on entering moves correctly.

Do I need Stockfish?

It works without it using a weaker built-in engine, but for the best advice download Stockfish and unzip it into the project folder. The app finds it automatically.

Is it free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license.

Can I use it in online games?

Check the rules of the site or tournament you play on. Many forbid outside engine assistance. It's best used for analysis, practice and over-the-board study.
